# FIRMS schema mapping (reviewed 2026-11-14)

NASA's MODIS and VIIRS FIRMS product descriptions document latitude, longitude, scan/track, acq_date, acq_time (UTC), satellite, confidence, version, FRP (MW) and daynight. MODIS brightness and bright_t31 are Kelvin bands; VIIRS bright_ti4 and bright_ti5 are Kelvin I-band observations and are not equivalent measurements. The MODIS satellite codes are T (Terra) and A (Aqua); VIIRS uses N (Suomi NPP), N20/1 (NOAA-20), N21/2 (NOAA-21). MODIS confidence is numeric 0–100; VIIRS confidence is L/N/H. Scan and track describe actual footprint dimensions in km. Preserve all source columns.

`latitude`/`longitude` → degrees WGS84; `acq_date`+`acq_time` → acquisition_datetime_utc; `satellite` → satellite; `confidence` → confidence_raw (sensor-specific); `version` → processing_version and documented suffix-derived processing_type; `frp` → frp_mw; `scan`/`track` → scan_km/track_km; `daynight` → daynight; `brightness` → brightness_modis_k; `bright_ti4`/`bright_ti5` → separate VIIRS bands; `type` → fire_type. Acquisition is overpass time, not ignition time. Extra columns remain in raw_record_json.

Sources: https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/content/descriptions/FIRMS_MODIS_Firehotspots.html ; https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/content/descriptions/FIRMS_VIIRS_Firehotspots.html ; https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/content/academy/data_ingest/firms_data_ingest.html ; https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/content/active_fire/ . Archive data is replaced by standard products as available; standard processing is preferred for retrospective analysis. NOAA-20 availability begins 2018-04-01; NOAA-21 begins 2024-01-17, outside the study period. Product availability does not imply full cloud-free coverage.

## Actual supplied archive variant — 2026-11-14
The actual ZIP files share `brightness`/`bright_t31` headers even for VIIRS; their `instrument` values distinguish `MODIS`, `VIIRS`, and `SNPP`. Full satellite names `Terra`, `Aqua`, and `SNPP` are accepted alongside short codes. NASA documents generic VIIRS `brightness` as I-4 in its service attributes: https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/content/descriptions/FIRMS_VIIRS_Firehotspots.html . The archive `bright_t31` field is preserved in raw attributes; its correspondence to I-5 in these specific archives is not yet independently verified, so it is not renamed to I-5. NOAA-20's suffix-free version is marked `unknown` in this implementation because the current FIRMS README states standard NOAA-20 products are unavailable despite the supplied archive naming: https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/download/Readme.txt . Do not silently treat it as equivalent to S-NPP or MODIS standard products.
