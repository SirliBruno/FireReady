# Scientific claims and source boundaries

## Verified source facts

- Hawaii State GIS publishes a Maui coastline and a statewide fire-station location layer. Existing dataset provenance and hashes are in the earlier sprint reports. The coastline derives from 1983 source geometry; it is not a surveyed current legal boundary. Professional station attributes do not establish vehicle inventory or 2023 operational status.
- The Hawaii AG official September 13, 2024 statement describes multiple interacting causes, including preparedness, infrastructure and incident coordination: https://ag.hawaii.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/News-Release-2024-40.pdf (pages 1–3).
- FSRI's official priority summary recommends communication resiliency (priority 4) and coordination of response preparedness/shared resources (priority 9): https://ag.hawaii.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/FSRI-Lahaina-Phase-2-Ten-Key-Actions-and-Priorities-Summary.pdf (PDF pages 5–6 and 8–9). No specific unverified pre-positioning finding is asserted in the UI. The rhetorical story hook is not a survivor quotation. No investigation database imagery was copied; its usage restrictions and privacy concerns were not bypassed.

## Scenario assumptions and results

One hypothetical vehicle per selected station, one required per simultaneous incident, direct projected distance to a selected cell representative point. Budget, thresholds, incident location snapping and reference policy are explicit. August example: three vehicles and 10 km threshold; weighted-demand coverage 44.4% reference and 87.0% optimum; weighted mean direct distance 11.38 and 6.97 km. Across 120 feasible allocations: median 61.1%, range 14.8–87.0%. These are fractions of historical demand proxy weight, not fires prevented, response-time gains or probability estimates.

## Exploratory findings

At a 20% land-area budget, annual/monthly validation capture was 46/52 and 37/52 local positive cell-days; 2023 capture was 75/131 and 47/131. Annual is now an exploratory starting recommendation informed by already-inspected evaluation. It is not a new independent test or universal model comparison. Stable zero-score tie breaking affects area-budget evaluation. Unknown observation coverage prevents claims about missed fires or calibrated risk.

## Must not be claimed

Verified source authenticity, independent perimeter/incident accuracy, measured emergency response times, road-network accessibility, actual Maui vehicle inventories, reconstructed 2023 deployment, universal annual/seasonal superiority, wildfire probability, casualties/economic loss prevented, or NASA endorsement. Hindsight exposure of 2023 is documented. Additional regions are not enabled without their own data/configuration. No road edges or incident histories are fabricated.
