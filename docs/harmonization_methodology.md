# Harmonization methodology

FireReady's exploratory harmonization means common analysis support and traceable candidate links, not sensor equivalence or confirmed fire incidents. The fixed reference uses MODIS Terra/Aqua and VIIRS Suomi NPP, all valid native confidence values, EPSG:32604 UTM zone 4N, 1,000 m grid, UTC-day bins, and all MODIS–VIIRS centroid pairs within 1,000 m and 60 minutes within the same evaluation partition. The 1 km nominal MODIS resolution motivates an interpretable starting scale; the distance and time are explicit exploratory assumptions, not calibrated fire-detection probabilities. MODIS pixel footprints vary with viewing geometry; a 500 m grid cannot add resolution. No precise sensor-footprint polygons are reconstructed without orientation information.

Grid cells are anchored to the UTM origin with floor indexing and clipped to the Maui coastline; only cells with selected observations are exported. Each cell-day holds original observation IDs, native confidence summaries, per-sensor FRP summaries and sensor counts. It contributes one positive grid-cell day regardless of detection count. Candidate edges preserve IDs, distance and time; multiple edges may refer to one observation. Edges are attributed to the MODIS observation's cell and UTC day, including possible across-day or adjacent-cell matches; unmatched means no candidate under thresholds, not a missed detection. Pair edges are not incident counts. Counts from different sensors remain available separately.

Sensitivity spans 500/1,000/2,000 m grids, 500/1,000/2,000 m distances, 30/60/180/360/1,440 min, two native quality policies and reference/all satellite cohorts (180 configurations). Only 2013–2022 observations enter this analysis. The reference was fixed a priori for interpretability, not optimized for overlap. A configuration lock binds the input dataset and partition hashes; sensitivity rejects a test-partition row. **2023 descriptive counts had already been seen during ingestion auditing before the lock.** Thresholds were not chosen from 2023 observations, but this is not fully blinded pre-registration.

Evidence: [NASA MODIS guide](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/s3fs-public/2023-09/MODIS_C6_C6.1_Fire_User_Guide_1.0.pdf), [NASA VIIRS description](https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/content/descriptions/FIRMS_VIIRS_Firehotspots.html), [Schroeder et al. 2014](https://directreadout.sci.gsfc.nasa.gov/publications_documents/Schroeder_et_al_2014b_RSE.pdf), and [Li et al. 2018](https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/49896/noaa_49896_DS1.pdf). The latter used stricter temporal and view-angle conditions for FRP comparison; our candidate thresholds are not adopted as their validated method. No independent coverage or ground truth is available.
