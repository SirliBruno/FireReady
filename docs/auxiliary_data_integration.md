# Auxiliary geographic data — final prototype

The existing Hawaii State GIS Maui coastline is the land boundary, with its documented 1983 source age and uncertainty. The official statewide fire station layer supplied ten eligible professional Maui station locations under the prototype's type/geographic filter. Retrieval date, source URL, 500 m boundary tolerance and historical/operational limitations are recorded in [Sprint 05](sprint_05_report.md) and [the final report](sprint_06_final_report.md).

No population, weather, vegetation, verified independent wildfire perimeters or road network is integrated. No exposure or weather estimates are inferred. Public road data availability was investigated, but a validated network/snapping matrix was not completed; the solver explicitly uses direct distance. Modern station points do not establish August 2023 deployment or real vehicle inventory.
