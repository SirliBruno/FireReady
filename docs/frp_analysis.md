# Fire radiative power

FRP is kept in megawatts and summarized separately by sensor and partition (count, minimum, maximum and median). Nulls remain null. No MODIS–VIIRS sum, fire energy, fire area or combined intensity is computed. Candidate FRP fields are preserved for inspection but not interpreted as equivalent measurements. Li et al. (2018) demonstrate dependence on conditions and use temporal/view-angle constraints in their comparison: https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/49896/noaa_49896_DS1.pdf . The necessary viewing-angle controls are not available in these CSVs.
