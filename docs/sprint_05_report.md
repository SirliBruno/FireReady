# Sprint 05 — functional hypothetical resource planning MVP

The MVP now provides a full clipped Maui land grid, train-only monthly demand, official station geography and an exact equal-budget maximum-coverage solver. This is not a complete scientific validation of preparedness recommendations.

## Actual inputs and model

2,039 positive-area 1 km UTM EPSG:32604 cells are generated by intersecting the frozen lattice with the existing Hawaii State GIS Maui coastline. The coastline hash is checked before use; its 1983 source and shoreline limitations remain. IDs preserve `1000:x:y` alignment. Unknown/unobserved cells are not classified safe. Only Maui is enabled.

The official statewide fire station layer was retrieved from https://geodata.hawaii.gov/arcgis/rest/services/EmergMgmtPubSafety/MapServer/7 on 2026-11-14. Ten Maui professional stations intersect the boundary or its 500 m tolerance. The raw layer is stored in `data/auxiliary/fire_stations_statewide.geojson`. The layer documents collection during 2025; historical 2023 eligibility and inventories are not verified. Military/volunteer sites are excluded by the explicit professional filter. Source attributes do not establish current dispatch availability.

Monthly demand weights count distinct training local cell-days divided by eight calendar training years; 397 training units reconcile to Sprint 04. Only observations labelled training by the existing UTC partition are used; validation and test are not used to build demand. Rates are calendar-normalized and not adjusted for unknown satellite opportunities. No vehicle requirement or wildfire probability is inferred. The generated model records a configuration hash; it is not a preregistration and no blinded holdout evaluation is claimed.

`public/data/planning/maui.json` includes the complete land grid, station geography, 12 demand vectors, projected straight-line cost matrix and model metadata. Rebuild with `.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m pipeline.planning` after the existing reference preview and government source file are available.

## Solver and comparison

An exact exhaustive subset solver maximizes weighted demand within a configurable straight-line kilometer radius, then minimizes weighted mean distance over all demand at a tie. Budget is 0–10 vehicles, at most one at each station. This is meaningful for geographic coverage: additional vehicles at the same station cannot improve this objective and are not allocated. Capacitated simultaneous incidents, reserve policies and multiple vehicles per station are not modelled.

The reference uses the same number of stations, selected evenly across longitude-sorted station positions. It is transparent, deterministic and hypothetical, not the actual Maui 2023 deployment. Both scenarios use identical weights, distances, thresholds and budget. The UI labels demand coverage as a share of weighted historical demand, never as a share of prevented fires. Weighted distance is in km, not measured driving or emergency response time. No road network has been acquired or used.

The functional browser run with August, three hypothetical vehicles and a 10 km direct-distance threshold displayed 44.4% reference weighted-demand coverage versus 87.0% proposed coverage, with weighted mean distances 11.38 km versus 6.97 km. These values apply only to this scenario and must not be generalized as an operational improvement.

## Exploratory historical ranking evaluation

A parameter lock is written to `data/processed/planning/evaluation_config.lock.json` before evaluation; it records the already-generated model ID, stable cell-ID tie breaking, methods and 10/20/30% land-area budgets. `python -m pipeline.planning_evaluation` compares annual versus monthly training activity on validation and 2023 positive local cell-days using the same full land grid. The uniform reference is an expected area fraction, not a fitted or sampled station deployment. Cells with zero training weight enter only to fill equal evaluation area, and deterministic tie breaking can materially affect these results; it is not evidence that those cells are safe.

At 20% land area, annual ranking captured 46/52 validation local cell-days (88.5%), monthly ranking 37/52 (71.2%), versus uniform expected 20%. For 2023, annual captured 75/131 (57.3%) and monthly 47/131 (35.9%). The annual baseline outperformed monthly at this area budget; **no claim of monthly superiority is justified**. The prototype retains monthly demand as a transparent user-selected exploratory scenario, not as a validated best model. Positive satellite observations are not independent wildfire ground truth, coverage is unknown, and earlier 2023 exposure prevents calling this evaluation fully blinded. No parameters were retuned after these results. Results are in `public/data/planning/historical_validation.json`. A locked model does not erase prior exposure. Independent perimeter validation and sensitivity to geographic tie breaking remain outstanding.
