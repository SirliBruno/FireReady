# Historical validation — final prototype

Annual and monthly train-only activity rankings were evaluated on validation (52 positive local cell-days) and 2023 retrospective observations (131). At 20% eligible land area: annual/monthly capture was 46/52 versus 37/52, and 75/131 versus 47/131 respectively. See `public/data/planning/historical_validation.json` and [final report](sprint_06_final_report.md).

The same complete land grid and area budgets are used. Stable zero-score tie breaking affects the selected evaluation area and is not evidence of low wildfire risk. Uniform comparison is an expected area fraction. The evaluation configuration is frozen locally before execution; prior exposure to 2023 descriptive results means the assessment is not fully blinded. No independent perimeter or incident validation has been completed. The annual exploratory default is informed by these already-inspected results, not a new independent test.
